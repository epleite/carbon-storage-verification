# AI-assisted research workflow

OpenAI ChatGPT assisted with:

- structured web-search planning and source triage;
- preliminary application of an author-defined evidence rubric;
- code development and checking;
- manuscript language and accessibility editing.

Two procedurally isolated AI-assisted scoring passes were used during development to identify records sensitive to search path or evidence-depth interpretation. They are **not** treated as ratings by independent human researchers, and historical pass agreement is **not** used as evidence of inter-rater reliability or objectivity in the final analysis.

The final workflow instead uses source-level auditability and explicit lower/adjudicated/upper defensible evidence bounds. On 2026-09-04 the sole author source-checked and confirmed all 45 final classifications without changes.

The author defined the scientific criteria, reviewed the retained evidence, approved all final classifications, set the ambiguity bounds, interpreted the analyses and assumes full responsibility for the manuscript and this release.
