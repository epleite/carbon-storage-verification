# Public-evidence rubric

## Claim axes

### K2 — injected/stored mass
Requires an observed CO2 mass or amount with a defined reporting period and an identifiable accounting boundary. Design capacity, nameplate capacity and planned injection do not qualify as observed mass.

### K3 — observed subsurface behaviour relative to expectations
Requires an observed quantitative subsurface response compared with a baseline, model or forecast. E2 or higher additionally requires an uncertainty statement, detection limit or sensitivity appropriate to the comparison.

### K4 — containment or migration-pathway evidence
Requires an observed quantitative result addressing a named containment pathway, subsurface irregularity or relevant migration question. E2 or higher additionally requires uncertainty, detection sensitivity or an explicit quantitative limit appropriate to the claim. Operational well/equipment integrity is not automatically equated with proof against all geological leakage pathways.

## Evidence depth

- **E0** — no qualifying evidence located after the standardized search;
- **E1** — plan, narrative, qualitative statement, or quantitative information insufficient for the claim-specific E2 rule;
- **E2** — auditable observed quantitative result satisfying the claim-specific rule;
- **E3** — public material sufficient for meaningful independent reanalysis;
- **E4** — deeper end-to-end reproducibility beyond the E3 threshold.

The manuscript's primary public-evidence threshold is **E2+**.

## Ambiguity treatment

Seven records differed across historical isolated scoring passes. After the complete retained-source set was reviewed, current lower/adjudicated/upper defensible scores were assigned from the evidence itself rather than from pass disagreement. Only two current bounds cross E1/E2: Snøhvit K4 and Dark Horse K2. All four independent threshold-level combinations are propagated in `data/ambiguity_sensitivity_results.csv`.
