# Data licence and third-party rights

Author-generated annotations, evidence classifications, ambiguity bounds, mapping labels and derived numerical tables in this repository are made available under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** licence, to the extent that the author holds rights in those annotations and derived expressions.

This licence does **not** relicense third-party material.

In particular:

- the March 2026 IEA CCUS Projects Database is not redistributed and remains subject to the IEA's terms;
- regulatory documents, operator reports, journal articles and public repository data remain subject to their respective source terms;
- URLs and factual identifiers are provided for provenance and auditability;
- users who retrieve third-party source material must comply with the source's own terms.

Source attribution and lineage are documented in `docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md`, `data/evidence_source_registry.csv`, and `data/geology_source_registry.csv`.
